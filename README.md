# CardDrop

Sea's namecard app: his 360TRUCK card with LINE QR and links to share, a scanner for other people's cards (read by the Gemini or Claude API with a key kept on the phone), the place and GPS where they met, a nice-to-meet-you email from Gmail with the card attached, photos in Google Drive (folder `CardDrop App`) and a row per person in the Namecards Google Sheet.

Open at https://chuchchon.github.io/carddrop/ and install it from Chrome.
